# Ground Texture Provenance

The starter coastal world uses locally generated, deterministic procedural textures and world-space shader variation for grass, exposed soil, sand and asphalt. The meadow material combines broad biome patches, medium-scale vegetation variation and fine ground grain so the terrain does not read as a single flat green plane. No runtime texture download is required, so the city, beach and road materials render offline and do not depend on third-party CORS headers or CDN uptime.

| Surface | Runtime material | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| Meadow grass | Local procedural texture plus world-space fractal noise, deep meadow, fresh grass, olive grass and exposed-soil blending | Generated by `WorldPresentation.gd` |
| Sandy beach | Local procedural sand texture | Generated by `WorldPresentation.gd` |
| City and highway asphalt | Local procedural albedo and normal maps | Generated by `WorldPresentation.gd` and `RoadSystem.gd` |

The following CC0 references were evaluated during development but are not fetched by the game at runtime:

- Wild Meadow Grass 2K — https://cdn.meshy.ai/landing-assets/textures/grass/wild-meadow-grass/wild-meadow-grass-2k.jpg
- Pitted Sandy Ground 2K — https://cdn.meshy.ai/landing-assets/textures/ground/pitted-sandy-ground/pitted-sandy-ground-2k.jpg
- Smooth Grey Asphalt 2K — https://cdn.meshy.ai/landing-assets/textures/concrete/smooth-grey-asphalt/smooth-grey-asphalt-2k.jpg

Do not add loose stones, twigs or vegetation to road geometry. Environmental scatter must exclude occupied road cells.
