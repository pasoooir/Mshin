City Building Guide

Drag to pan. Use the mouse wheel or a two-finger pinch to zoom. Select Road and tap empty plots to build roads. Buildings require road access. Designate Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Administrative and Recreation zones; the city develops them as demand grows.

Citizens move through the city, vehicles use roads, and the treasury changes with income and service costs. Build power, water, sewage, waste, police, fire, healthcare and education facilities to improve service coverage. Control simulation speed at the bottom. Your city is saved on this device.
