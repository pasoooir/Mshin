# SimCity BuildIt level-up reference and Mshin progression plan

**Reference checked:** 2026-11-07  
**Primary source:** [SimCity BuildIt Wiki — Level Up Table](https://simcity-buildit.fandom.com/wiki/Level_Up_Table)  
**Supporting sources:** [Residential Buildings](https://simcity-buildit.fandom.com/wiki/Residential_Buildings), [Services](https://simcity-buildit.fandom.com/wiki/Services), [EA beginner guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/simcity/simcity-buildit/beginner-guide/).

> This is a gameplay reference, not a claim that Mshin should reproduce SimCity BuildIt assets or proprietary mechanics one-to-one. Mshin should use its own models, names, economy and balance. The level table is community-maintained and may lag the live game.

## Findings

- The table contains unlocks through **level 87**. Its note says item unlocks stop after level 87 and building/feature unlocks stop after level 94.
- **Levels 88–94 have no listed new unlocks** in the table; level 95+ has no new unlocks according to its note.
- Unlocks are split across materials, commercial production buildings, factories, city buildings and other systems.
- The major service gates are: Fire at 5, Sewage at 8, Police at 12, Waste Management at 14 and Health at 16.
- Education and Transportation specializations begin at 10; Beach at 15; Mountain at 23; OMEGA at 30; Space-related content at 40.
- Residential growth is not simply an automatic level reward: the EA guide describes placing a residential zone beside a road, collecting requested items, and then completing the construction/upgrade. Mshin should preserve the visible staged construction workflow.

## Mshin progression phases

| Mshin phase | Reference levels | Unlock themes to adapt | Mshin implementation direction |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1. Founding | 1–4 | Roads, starter homes, basic factory, building supplies, first parks and storage | Empty buildable land; road tool; starter residential plots; basic production and storage; early decorative parks. |
| 2. Essential services | 5–9 | Fire, sewage, mayoral buildings, landscape items, four-lane roads | Fire coverage, sewage capacity, emergency response, first road-capacity upgrade. Warn before a level unlock adds an unmet service demand. |
| 3. City services & mobility | 10–16 | Education, transport, police, waste, health, six-lane roads, beach and epic projects | Schools, transit, police, waste and clinics with coverage/capacity; explain effects on happiness; allow road upgrades. |
| 4. Commerce & districts | 17–24 | Clubs, donut/fashion shops, entertainment, mountain specialization, more factory capacity | Expand production chains, commercial storefronts, leisure, mountain district and richer residential visual tiers. |
| 5. Regions & OMEGA | 25–39 | Regional zones/factories, boulevard roads, OMEGA labs/zones, Neobank, streetcar and Maglev roads | Region unlock progression, specialized production, high-density OMEGA district and advanced transit. |
| 6. Landmarks & space | 40–60 | Space HQ, observatory, satellite center, restoration, astronaut training, launch pad, mission control | Landmark district, restoration gameplay, space campus, launch infrastructure and visually distinctive civic buildings. |
| 7. Late-game production | 61–87 | Advanced commercial recipes and shop tiers; late items such as churros, profiterole, apple jam, mochi and pavlova | Advanced goods chains, shop upgrades, longer production timers, trade/warehouse depth and late-game cosmetic architecture. |
| 8. Post-unlock progression | 88–94 and 95+ | No further unlocks listed after the table's stated cutoffs | Continue through mastery, building upgrades, city challenges, regions, visual variants and economy goals rather than adding arbitrary level gates. |

### Asset ingestion status

No third-party screenshot or game artwork has been copied into this repository in this commit. The browser/search environment returned source pages and image URLs but did not provide downloadable image bytes or per-file licence metadata. Rehosting those files as if cleared would risk violating copyright. The index above deliberately links each visual reference to its source and distinguishes reusable-candidate Fandom files from third-party reference-only screenshots.

For repository-owned visuals, create original Mshin models from the reference categories (not copied game meshes or screenshots). If exact Fandom files are later downloaded, preserve the file page, creator, licence, source URL and attribution in a per-asset manifest; CC BY-SA assets must retain compatible share-alike terms.

## Implementation implications for Mshin

1. Use the level gates as a **progression reference**, not as a direct copy of SimCity BuildIt.
2. Keep the first-launch city empty and fully buildable as required by Mshin's current product direction.
3. Introduce each new service only with a clear demand forecast, affordability warning and coverage preview.
4. Tie unlocks to visible catalogue cards and their own approved image/model asset; no generic cube or unrelated thumbnail.
5. Use the actual staged construction loop: plot → site clearing/foundation → workers/equipment → structure shell → finished building → upgrade variants.
6. Treat roads, services, production buildings and residential growth as connected systems, not isolated level rewards.
7. Keep all runtime assets local/offline, with source/licence/provenance metadata.

## Accuracy and licensing caveats

- The level table is community-maintained; verify any disputed or recent unlock against the live game before balancing Mshin.
- Several source entries use shorthand names (for example, “Lake”, “Regional commercial building” or “OMEGA Maglev roads”); retain their original labels in the data source rather than inventing more precise names.
- Do not copy SimCity BuildIt logos, UI, proprietary 3D models or screenshots into Mshin without permission.
